Get in touch

Need help with TOSS! for iOS? Contact Alena Martinková at hello@martinkova.dev.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary personal information.

02 /

Skin packs and coins

Open Skins & shop to find paper, ball and sock skins. Premium packs unlock two skins each; coin packs add 300 or 900 coins. Prices appear in your local App Store currency before purchase. There are no subscriptions.

To restore a paid skin pack, use Restore purchases in the shop or Settings with the Apple account used to buy it. Coin balances, skins unlocked with coins and progress stay on the device; restoring purchases does not recover them after deleting app data or switching phones.

If a purchase is awaiting approval, it appears when Apple approves it. For a missing purchase, check your connection and Apple purchase history, then contact support. Refund requests are handled by Apple at Report a Problem.

03 /

Optional ads

Rewarded ads are your choice. The button shows the reward before you start: coins, a bonus after completing a challenge, or an extra attempt. Leaving before earning the reward does not grant the bonus. When a video is unavailable, keep playing and try again later.

Where available, change your advertising choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Read the privacy policy for details.

04 /

Your progress and settings

The game works offline and keeps progress on your iPhone. There is no game account or automatic cross-device progress sync. Deleting the app’s data removes local progress, coins and coin skins.

Settings lets you change sound, haptics and gentler motion. The app respects the silent switch and the device’s Reduce Motion setting. Each skin applies to its object: paper, ping-pong ball or sock. Skins change appearance, not physics.
