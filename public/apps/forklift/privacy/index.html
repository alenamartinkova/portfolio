Who looks after your data?

Forklift Certified for iOS is provided by Alena Martinková, the controller responsible for the processing described here. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@martinkova.dev.

This policy covers the native iOS app and this privacy page. It does not describe the separate browser game on this website.

02 /

What stays on your iPhone?

The game saves your completed shifts, ratings and best times, coins, achievements, unlocked cosmetics, forklift appearance and custom nameplate, saved looks, settings and language on your device. It also saves completed-run and rewarded-video identifiers to prevent duplicate rewards, plus an ad counter and last-ad time to space out interruptions.

These saves are not uploaded to a game server. There is no game account, multiplayer profile, or custom cloud-sync service. iOS device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The free game and local saves work offline; new purchases, restores and loading new ads require a connection.

03 /

Advertising and your choices

When advertising is enabled, the app uses Google AdMob and Google’s consent service. Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google’s SDK data disclosure.

The app disables ad personalization and does not request Apple’s tracking permission. This does not stop all advertising-related processing. Google’s consent screen collects choices where required; you can revisit Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is available. More about Google’s use of information from partner apps.

Reward videos are optional. Buying Remove Ads stops automatic ads between shifts; optional reward videos remain available, and the advertising/consent services may still process data. If an ad is unavailable, you can keep playing. The app has no separate analytics SDK.

04 /

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes in-app payments. The app reads verified purchase information, such as product and transaction identifiers and ownership or refund status, to unlock purchases and restore them. Your payment-card details are not provided to the game.

Apple may make sales reports and, depending on your device-sharing choices, app diagnostics available to the developer. Apple handles its own account and transaction records under App Store & Privacy.

05 /

If you get in touch

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include, such as a description of a problem or a screenshot. We use it to respond to your request and keep relevant correspondence only as long as needed to resolve it, handle related issues or meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

06 /

This page and service providers

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded third-party media or tracking cookies. Language switching uses ordinary links and does not save a preference. The page reads the portfolio’s saved colour theme and accent from local storage; changing the theme saves that display preference on your device. These settings are not sent to us.

The website is hosted on Netlify. Delivering and protecting the site can involve technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and time. See Netlify’s privacy information and its data-processing agreement. Support correspondence is processed through the email services used to send and receive it.

Apple, Google, website hosting and email providers process information needed for their respective services. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. There is no separate sale of your local game save.

07 /

Storage, deletion and international processing

Local progress stays on your device until you remove the app and its data. In iOS storage settings, choose Delete App; offloading an app keeps its data. Manage device backups separately in your Apple settings. Deletion does not cancel or erase Apple purchases, and restoring a backup may restore saved progress.

We cannot remotely read or delete a save held only on your device. Apple and Google keep records according to their own purposes and retention policies; deleting the game does not automatically remove those records. See Google’s retention information.

Service providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their policies describe applicable safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. See Google’s transfer safeguards and Apple’s privacy policy. Contact us for information about transfers relating to data we control.

08 /

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, the grounds we rely on are providing the game or a requested purchase, consent where required for advertising, legitimate interests in answering support requests and keeping the website secure, and applicable legal obligations.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing. Contact hello@martinkova.dev; for provider-held data, their privacy links explain further options.

The game makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

09 /

For parents and guardians

The app has no registration, in-game chat, or photo-upload feature and does not ask players to provide an age. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address the request. Use the device’s parental controls to manage purchases and access to apps.

10 /

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Where required, significant changes will be brought to your attention in the app or through a renewed consent request.
